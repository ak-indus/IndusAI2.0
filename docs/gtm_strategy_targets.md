## Part 2: Target Segmentation

### Tier 1 — "Day 1 Targets" (Specialty Chemical Distributors, $50M-$250M)
> **Why first:** Shortest sales cycle, daily pain, small enough to move fast, big enough to pay.
> **Deal size:** $3-8K/month ($36-96K ACV)
> **Sales motion:** Founder-led, warm intro via NACD/ACD network, demo-to-close in 2-4 weeks

### Tier 2 — "Month 3 Targets" (Heavy-Ops MRO Distributors, $100M-$500M)
> **Why second:** Larger back-office teams = bigger savings, but longer sales cycles.
> **Deal size:** $5-15K/month ($60-180K ACV)
> **Sales motion:** Champion in operations/IT, prove ROI on one workflow, expand

### Tier 3 — "Month 4-6 Targets" (Mid-Size Chemical Suppliers/Manufacturers)
> **Why third:** Bigger budgets, but enterprise procurement. Need case studies from Tier 1.
> **Deal size:** $10-25K/month ($120-300K ACV)
> **Sales motion:** Sell to VP of Sales or VP of Technical Services. "Your reps answer the same 50 questions 100x/day. We automate that."

## Part 4: Execution Playbook

### Phase 1 — Weeks 1-4: Land First 2-3 Chemical Distributors

**Actions:**
1. **Build target list of 20 contacts** across Tier 1 companies
   - Title targets: VP of Sales, Director of Customer Service, VP of Operations, CTO/IT Director
   - Source via LinkedIn Sales Navigator + ACD/NACD membership directories
2. **Craft the outbound sequence:**
   - Email 1: "Your customers email you 50 product questions/day. What if AI answered in 30 seconds?"
   - Email 2: Show 90-second demo video (the TDS/SDS lookup → instant response flow)
   - Email 3: ROI calculator — "With X reps spending Y hours on lookups, you're spending $Z/year. We cost 10% of that."
3. **Offer free 2-week pilot** on their actual catalog:
   - Ingest their product catalog in 48 hours (4-stage pipeline)
   - Set up email triage on a shadow inbox (AI drafts responses, human approves)
   - Track: queries handled, time saved, accuracy rate
4. **Convert pilot to paid** at $3-5K/month

**Week 1 Priority Targets (Start Here):**

| Company | Contact Title to Find | Opening Line |
|---------|----------------------|--------------|
| Sea-Land Chemical | VP of Sales or Director of Customer Service | "I noticed Sea-Land handles specialty chemical inquiries across [X] product lines. What if your team could answer every customer in 30 seconds instead of 30 minutes?" |
| H.M. Royal | Technical Sales Manager | "Rubber and polymer cross-referencing is one of the most knowledge-intensive lookups in chemical distribution. We built AI specifically for this." |
| Chemsolv | VP of Operations | "Specialty chemical distributors your size typically have 15-30 people handling order entry and product inquiries. We can automate 60% of that volume." |

### Phase 2 — Weeks 5-12: Expand to 5-8 Distributors + First MRO Target

**Actions:**
1. **Use Tier 1 case study** ("Sea-Land reduced inquiry response time by 90%") to approach remaining Tier 1 targets
2. **Begin Tier 2 outreach** to MRO distributors with the strongest cross-reference pain:
   - Bossard (fasteners) — our entity extraction is purpose-built for this
   - FCX Performance (flow control) — spec matching across pumps/valves
   - R.S. Hughes (adhesives) — compatibility matrix is a graph problem
3. **Attend one industry event:**
   - **ACD Annual Meeting** (specialty chemical distributors) — ~400 attendees, all decision-makers
   - **ISA (Industrial Supply Association) Convention** — MRO distributor buyers
   - **DCAT Week** (Drug, Chemical & Associated Technologies) — supplier sales teams

### Phase 3 — Months 4-6: Land First Supplier Deal

**Actions:**
1. **Approach Tier 3 suppliers** with: "We're already deployed at [X] distributors selling your products. Let us power AI across your entire distribution channel."
2. **The supplier pitch is different:**
   - Distributors buy for operational efficiency (save time/money)
   - Suppliers buy for **channel enablement** (make every distributor rep an expert on their products)
   - Frame: "Your competitors' distributors will have AI. Your distributors won't. Unless you deploy this."
3. **Start with one supplier segment:**
   - Best first target: **Stepan Company** (surfactants) — clear product-application matching problem, manageable portfolio size, sells through the same distributors we're already in
